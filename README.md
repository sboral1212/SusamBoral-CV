## Professional Profile

I am a Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin with a PhD
in Ocean Engineering and a background in applied mathematics,
numerical methods and computational research.

My work focuses on developing mathematical models, numerical
algorithms and simulation tools to understand complex physical
systems and solve engineering problems.

I have experience in fluid mechanics, structural dynamics,
scientific computing, machine learning and quantum algorithms.
My research combines mathematical analysis with computational
methods, with particular emphasis on model accuracy, numerical
efficiency and practical applications.

I have authored or co-authored more than 20 peer-reviewed
journal articles and contributed to international research
collaborations, scientific software development and teaching.

## Selected Research and Software Projects

### 1. Dynamic Modelling of Submarine Power Cables

**Trinity College Dublin | SEAI-funded InDyCORE Project**

Developing numerical methods to investigate the static
and dynamic behaviour of submarine power cables.

The work involves nonlinear differential equations,
hydrodynamic loading, numerical simulation and
comparison of independent modelling approaches.

**Tools:** MATLAB, OpenFAST, MoorDyn

**Related publication:**

Proceedings of the Royal Society A (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2025.0498

### 2. Machine Learning for Floating Elastic Structures

**Applied Mathematics and Computation (2026)**

Developed analytical and neural network models to
investigate the dynamic response of a floating elastic
plate supported by a viscoelastic foundation.

The work combines mathematical modelling, numerical
methods and machine learning to predict physical
responses and assess model accuracy.

**Tools:** Python, PyTorch, MATLAB

**Research project:**

https://sboral1212.github.io/floating-plate-viscoelastic-foundation/

**Publication:**

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amc.2025.129872

### 3. Python–MATLAB Research Simulation API

Developed a Python-based web application to make
MATLAB numerical simulations accessible to external
researchers through a browser.

**Key features:**

- Remote simulation submission
- Automated MATLAB execution
- Job identification and progress tracking
- Time-limited access tokens
- CSV, JSON and MATLAB data retrieval
- Automated graphical outputs
- Browser-based interface

**Technologies:** Python, Flask, MATLAB, REST API

**Research software prototype:**

https://crock-dreadlock-trowel.ngrok-free.dev/

*The interface is accessible when the research server
and ngrok tunnel are running.*

### 4. Quantum Algorithms for Differential Equations

**Trinity College Dublin | Ongoing Research**

Investigating quantum numerical algorithms for solving
differential equations, including:

- Kacewicz quantum algorithms
- Hybrid quantum–classical numerical methods
- Quantum circuit formulations
- Computational methods for nonlinear differential equations

**Tools:** MATLAB, Python, Qiskit
